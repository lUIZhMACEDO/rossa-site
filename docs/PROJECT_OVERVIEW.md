# rossa
ROSSA stands for the Rodent Open Science Skeletal Archive. It is a repository dedicated to the archiving and sharing of skeletal phenotyping data performed on rodent (rats and mice) animal models.  Currently, in this repository we are designing a data ingestion process to archive skeletal phenotyping datasets where the impact of a genetic change, drug treatment, procedure, or some combination thereof was studied for its ability to alter bone tissue.  Technical skeletal phenotyping approaches that will be ingested include Dual Energy X-Ray Absorptiometry (DEXA), Micro-Computed Tomagraphy (μCT), Bone Histomorphometry, Mechanical Strength Testing, and clinical biochemistry assays.
